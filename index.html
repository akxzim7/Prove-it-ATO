SENDING TO AN UNKNOWN SERVER
Classroom guide

For Teachers

Prove It is a 5-minute interactive simulator that teaches students to verify suspicious tax and super messages through official ATO channels.

Year levelYear 10–12
Time required15 minutes total (5 min simulator + 10 min class discussion)
SetupNone. No login, no app install, no student data collected. Works on mobile and school networks.

Learning outcomes

  • Identify common ATO impersonation scams across SMS, email, phone and app notifications.
  • Verify suspicious contact independently through the ATO app or my.gov.au instead of trusting the message.
  • Explain why checks the caller controls — caller ID, employee ID numbers — prove nothing.
  • Locate refund status, account balances and security messages in official ATO channels.
  • Describe what to do if an account is compromised, including locking an ATO record and who to phone.
